# Exploratory Testing Session – JPetStore

## Testing Goal

Explore product pages and validation behavior to identify defects affecting usability and content quality.

---

## Areas Tested

- product catalog
- product details pages
- field validation
- image loading
- product descriptions

---

## Observations

- Some product pages contain broken images.
- Certain product descriptions are incorrect or incomplete.
- Validation behavior should be improved on some fields.
- General navigation works correctly, but content quality issues reduce trust.

---

## Testing Notes

Testing was conducted using exploratory techniques to simulate typical user behavior when browsing products and interacting with the application.

The session focused on finding visible defects that could negatively affect user experience.
