# IMA-3 – Scrollbar indicator jumps from center to bottom when scrolling list in the “In theatres near you“ tab

## Steps to reproduce

1. Open the “IMBd“ app
2. Open tab “In theatres near you“
3. Scroll down watching the scrollbar indicator

## Expected result

Scrollbar indicator moves smoothly and proportionally to the scroll position

## Actual result

Scrollbar indicator jumps to the bottom from the center

## Priority

Medium
